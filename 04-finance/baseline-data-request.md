# Baseline Data Request

## Purpose

Before the operating team makes promises about growth, profitability, acquisition value, or investor readiness, the first owner-partner business needs a baseline.

## Required Financial Data

Request at least:

- trailing 36 months profit and loss statements
- trailing 36 months revenue by month
- trailing 36 months gross margin if available
- current year-to-date profit and loss
- balance sheet
- tax returns if available and appropriate under NDA
- owner compensation and benefits
- debt schedule
- accounts receivable aging
- accounts payable aging
- active backlog
- open estimates
- completed-job list for the last 12 months

## Required Operating Data

- lead sources
- call and form volume
- estimate appointments
- estimates sent
- estimate close rate
- average project value
- project duration by job type
- crew or subcontractor list
- job-cost records
- material cost trends
- callback or warranty issue list
- customer reviews
- software and systems currently used

## Required Legal And Risk Data

This is a checklist for professional review, not legal advice.

- entity documents
- licenses
- insurance policies
- customer contract templates
- subcontractor agreements
- employee or contractor records
- open disputes
- liens
- warranties
- vehicle and equipment list
- lease obligations

## Baseline Output

The finance pass should produce:

- normalized revenue
- normalized gross margin
- normalized SDE or owner earnings
- recurring versus one-time revenue notes
- margin by job type if possible
- cash conversion risks
- owner dependency notes
- acquisition value range assumptions
- 12 to 18 month improvement model
