# First Owner Call Script

## Purpose

Help Mikolaj, Edward, and Filip conduct early owner conversations consistently.

## Tone

- respectful
- curious
- low pressure
- owner-first
- no acquisition assumptions
- no valuation discussion before baseline

## Opening

Thanks for taking the time. We are speaking with respected construction business owners who may eventually want to reduce their day-to-day burden or think through succession. We are not here to push a sale. We are trying to understand what owners actually want and whether there is a way to help before any bigger conversation.

## Owner Goals

- What would you like your role in the business to look like 12 to 24 months from now?
- Are there parts of the business you still enjoy?
- Are there parts you would gladly hand off if you trusted the right people?
- Have you thought about succession or eventual retirement?
- Do you have family or internal people who want to take over?

## Business Reality

- What kind of work does the business do best?
- Where do most leads come from today?
- What usually limits growth?
- Is the hard part leads, people, scheduling, estimating, cash flow, or something else?
- What does a great customer look like?
- What type of job do you wish you did less often?

## Trust And Protection

- What would make you comfortable letting someone help with operations?
- What would make you uncomfortable?
- What do you want protected no matter what?
- How important are employees, brand, customer relationships, and your personal reputation in any transition?

## Possible Next Step

If there is mutual interest, the next step is not a purchase offer. The next step is usually a deeper conversation about the business, what support would be useful, and what information would be needed to see whether a small operating pilot makes sense.

## Internal Debrief

After the call, record:

- owner goals
- transition motivation
- trust level
- business type
- apparent systems gaps
- potential legal or financial risks
- next step
- assumptions updated
- open questions
