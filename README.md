# Construction Operating Group Plan

This repository is the working blueprint for building a repeatable construction operating group through hands-on partnerships with retiring or succession-constrained trade business owners.

The initial concept is to pair an operating team with an experienced construction business owner who wants to step back over time. The operating team improves sales, marketing, quoting, project management, people systems, and financial discipline over a 12 to 18 month launch period. If trust and performance develop, the team may pursue a seller-financed acquisition, long-term owner employment, or another mutually attractive transition.

## What This Is

This is not one finished business plan document. It is a structured planning repository.

Use it to:

- understand the business thesis
- challenge assumptions
- assign work to humans and agents
- prepare owner/referral conversations
- build a first 90-day launch process
- eventually produce investor-ready materials

## Working Rules

- Keep canonical plan content in the numbered folders.
- Use `09-contributor-workspace/` for rough notes and drafts.
- Do not bury business decisions only in `.agents/`.
- Add assumptions explicitly before building plans on top of them.
- Prefer systems over goals: every target should map to a repeatable operating mechanism.
- Use Pareto thinking: identify the few control points that drive most risk reduction and value creation.
- Design incentives so owners, operators, workers, customers, and investors can all win through repeated cooperation rather than one-time extraction.

## Current Success Horizon

The first serious test is a 12 to 18 month launch with one owner-partner business. The working target is to improve revenue by roughly 25 percent and profitability by roughly 20 percent, subject to validation against baseline financials, trade type, market, backlog, staffing, and owner participation.
