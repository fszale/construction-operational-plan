# Launch Sequence Maintenance

## Process

1. Read `07-execution-plan/30-60-90-launch-plan.md`.
2. Read `07-execution-plan/first-version-readiness-checklist.md`.
3. Read `00-overview/open-questions.md` and `07-execution-plan/decision-log.md`.
4. Update launch actions, owners, exit criteria, and timing.
5. Keep unknown commitments and unvalidated assumptions visible.
6. Add material launch choices to the decision log.

## Launch Sequence Standard

The plan should always answer:

- what to do now
- what to do next
- what to defer
- who owns each workstream
- what evidence gates the next stage
- what would cause a pivot or stop
