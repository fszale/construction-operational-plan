# Investor Storytelling

## Process

1. State the market problem in operator language.
2. Explain why retiring owner-operated businesses create an opportunity.
3. Show the operating team's value creation system.
4. Make the first 12 to 18 month proof plan concrete.
5. Separate actual traction from planned traction.
6. List evidence needed before fundraising.

## Guardrails

- Do not overstate acquisition certainty.
- Do not imply securities, legal, or tax conclusions.
- Keep claims evidence-backed or clearly labeled as assumptions.
