# Digital Presence

## Standard

The business should look like a serious, modern, trustworthy construction company online. The customer should quickly understand what the company does, where it operates, what quality looks like, how to request a consultation, and why the team can be trusted.

## Initial Market Focus

Digital presence should be built around Southeast Michigan search behavior and service-area credibility, especially in wealthier Detroit suburbs such as Macomb, Chesterfield, Rochester, Shelby Township, Bloomfield, Commerce, Novi, Northville, and adjacent high-income communities.

Each service page should eventually support local proof: project photos, reviews, city references, service-area pages, and calls to action for consultations.

## Reference Pattern

Matrix Basements is a useful external benchmark because its public website emphasizes:

- clear service category
- consultation request flow
- visual project inspiration
- financing and promotional offers
- reviews and customer proof
- locations and service areas
- awards, certifications, and trust badges
- service-specific landing pages

This project should use those patterns as a quality bar, not as copy.

## Initial Website Sections

- homepage
- services
- project gallery
- before and after examples
- consultation request
- reviews
- about the team
- service areas
- financing or payment options if available
- FAQ
- careers

## Marketing System

Track:

- website visits
- form submissions
- calls
- lead source
- appointment booking rate
- estimate show rate
- close rate
- cost per lead
- cost per sold job
- review generation
