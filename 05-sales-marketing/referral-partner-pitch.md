# Referral Partner Pitch

## Purpose

Explain the concept to people who may know owner-operated construction businesses: accountants, attorneys, insurance agents, suppliers, brokers, lenders, trade associations, and local business contacts.

## Short Version

We are looking to meet respected construction business owners who may want to step back over time but do not have a clear succession path.

We are not looking for distressed companies or quick-flip acquisitions. We are looking for good operators with real local reputation where additional systems, marketing, project management, financial visibility, and people processes could help the owner reduce burden and create a better transition path.

## Good Referral Profile

- aging or tired owner
- strong trade reputation
- no clear family successor
- business still has customer demand
- owner cares about employees and customers
- administrative burden is heavy
- digital presence or systems are underdeveloped
- owner may be open to gradual transition

## Bad Referral Profile

- severe reputation damage
- active legal mess
- unsafe operating culture
- owner refuses transparency
- distressed fire sale
- business depends on one customer or one unreliable person

## Referral Ask

If you know an owner like this, we would appreciate an introduction. The first conversation is exploratory and respectful. We are trying to understand the owner's goals before discussing any structure.
